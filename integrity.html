Academic integrity

Cite the tutor. Keep the thinking.

Integrity is matching the name on the work to the mind that did it. Using a model as a quiz partner can be honest. Using it as a ghostwriter is not, even if you “tweak a few words.”

What you can usually say yes to

  • Explanations, analogies, and Socratic questions
  • Similar practice that is not the assigned item
  • Structure checklists and rubric decoding
  • Feedback on an outline or draft you wrote

What this site will not help you do

  • Full speeches, papers, lab write-ups, or code solutions
  • Answer keys
  • “Make it sound like a student”
  • Translating the whole assignment into a submission

A disclosure you can actually turn in

I used an AI tutor to decode the assignment, quiz my outline, and check that I could explain the ideas. I did not use it to generate the work I am submitting. Sources I cite were found and read by me.

Your Learning Plan copies a version tailored to the assignment type. If your teacher wants a specific form (MLA note, syllabus appendix), follow that.

Citations are still on you

Models invent sources. If you did not open the article, you cannot cite it. “The chatbot said so” is not a bibliography entry.

When policy is restricted or you are unsure, ask before you paste a full draft. Privacy: skip personal data and unpublished work that isn’t yours to share.
