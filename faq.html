FAQ

Straight answers

Will this write my assignment if I phrase it nicely?

No. Not as a speech, paper, lab, function, or “short version.” The studio builds a Learning Plan. Tutor mode refuses RED requests.

Do I need an account?

No. The last assignment and plan save in localStorage on this browser. On a shared computer, clear it when you leave.

Is there a live AI model behind the plan?

Not in this version. Plans are high-quality templates filled from your assignment type and keywords. The note on the results page says so. A live model could be added later with the same hard rule: never produce the deliverable.

What if I don’t have the brief?

Choose “here’s what I remember.” Write the task, length, and what the teacher said “good” looks like. Too-short pastes get a warning so the plan doesn’t fake specificity.

Can I paste a draft?

Yes — your writing only. You get a feedback checklist (clarity, evidence, structure), not a rewritten submission.

My teacher banned all AI.

Follow the teacher. Use the decode and try-first sections without copying prompts into an outside chatbot. Mark policy as Restricted so the plan reminds you.

What should I be able to say about AI after this?

Generative AI predicts likely text; it does not understand like a person. Fluent answers can still be empty of your thinking. Check citations. Don’t paste private stuff.

Who is this for?

High school and first-year college, in a calm study-lounge tone. We won’t shame you for being tempted to skip. We also won’t help you skip.
